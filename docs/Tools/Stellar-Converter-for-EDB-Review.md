## What Is Stellar Converter for EDB?

Stellar Converter for EDB is designed to convert Exchange Database (EDB) files into Outlook-compatible PST files. It supports both **offline** and hosted **online** EDB files, allowing IT teams to preview and export mailbox data. With support for multiple Exchange Server versions, it provides a practical way to extract mailbox content without relying entirely on native Exchange tools.

## How It Works in Practice

In practice, the workflow is simple and focused. For this review, the software was tested with an offline EDB file.

1. **Launch the application.**
2. **Load the offline .edb file** – the scanner identifies mailboxes and folders.
3. **Preview and select** – browse the mailbox tree and preview items before export.
4. **Export to PST** – choose the mailboxes or folders to export and create Outlook-compatible PST files.

That export-focused workflow reflects the product's design: keep the process simple, reliable, and predictable.

## Technical Assessment

One of the tool's strengths is how effectively it hides Exchange database complexity behind a straightforward interface. In testing, it handled three technical areas particularly well:

- **EDB parsing** – reads the database structure and identifies mailboxes reliably.
- **Item reconstruction** – preserves message headers, timestamps, attachments, and folder structure.
- **PST generation** – creates files that import cleanly into Outlook and Exchange Online.

In testing, Stellar Converter for EDB preserved metadata and attachments consistently, and performance on modern hardware was solid. The interface also keeps the experience approachable, making it usable even for generalist IT admins without deep Exchange expertise.

## Pros

- **Reliable conversion** – handles offline and hosted EDB files and converts full mailbox data, including emails, contacts, calendars, and attachments.
- **No Exchange dependency** – can open and export offline EDB data without requiring a running Exchange server, which is useful for decommissioned servers.
- **Direct export options** – supports direct export to Microsoft 365 and live Exchange, which can save time compared with PST-only workflows.
- **Granular selection and filtering** – lets you target specific mailboxes, folders, or items and apply filters such as date or sender, which is helpful for focused recovery or compliance work.
- **Exports Public Folder** – Export Mailboxes to Public Folders on Exchange or Office 365.
- **Good data integrity** – preserves folder structure, metadata, and attachments during export.
- **Parallel processing** – can process 8 mailboxes at the same time, which improves performance for medium-scale jobs.
- **User-friendly interface** – the GUI is easier to work with than script-heavy workflows, especially for junior engineers or urgent support cases.
- **Support for large EDB files** – is well positioned to handle large databases without the size limits often found in simpler tools.

Overall, Stellar Converter for EDB is most effective when the goal is mailbox conversion, extraction, or selective export with minimal setup and a lower learning curve than native Exchange methods.

## Cons

- **Limited free version** – the free edition is mainly for preview, so it is not enough for real conversion or one-off validation work.
